Mussa Mushi — Expedition Guide, Expedition Leader & Tour Consultant

With more than 20 years of experience in Tanzania's mountains, wilderness and tourism, Mussa Mushi has helped thousands of travelers pursue unforgettable adventures, including reaching Uhuru Peak, the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Mussa has also led and supported expeditions to Mount Meru, Mount Ol Doinyo Lengai and Mount Kenya, while bringing the same passion and experience to Tanzania safari adventures and Zanzibar beach holidays.

While our professional team is ready to take care of your journey, some guests prefer the opportunity to have Mussa personally lead their expedition or safari.
